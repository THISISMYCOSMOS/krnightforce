# car-club

A car club card that shows the car model and "held for a year or more", and hides the plate number, purchase month and owner. Planned from a one-line request; walkthrough: `docs/design-guide/walkthrough-car-club.md`. The holed version has two easy mistakes. First, the duplicate-plate check hashes the plate alone, so the engine recovers the plate (A2, criterion 4). Second, the one-year check uses `blockTimeGte` on the purchase time. The compiler asks for `disclose()` there, and wrapping it compiles but puts the time on chain, so the engine recovers the purchase month (A2 against the leakage description, criterion 6). The fixed version mixes the operator's secret into the plate tag and compares against a public club month. The record holds the member key `owner_key_of(owner_secret)`; the engine finds that relation by itself during setup. The scenario's plate range is the last four digits; a real plate space (about 3.6e8) is still enumerable offline.
